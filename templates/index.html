How It Works

From health parameters to a risk estimate in three steps.

1

Enter the parameters

Fill in the 13 health measurements the model was trained on, from age and blood pressure to exercise test results.

2

The model analyses them

Your values are scaled the same way as the training data, then passed to a trained machine-learning classifier.

3

Read the risk estimate

The model's probability is shown as a percentage and grouped into a low, moderate or high risk band.

About This Project

An educational machine-learning project built for a workshop.

CardioPredict trains a classifier on a classic heart disease dataset and wraps it in a web app, so you can see how a model turns patient measurements into a probability.

It shows how a full machine-learning workflow fits together: data, training, saving the model, and serving predictions through a web interface.

  • DatasetUCI Heart Disease (Cleveland)
  • Records303 patients
  • Input features13
  • TaskBinary classification

Not medical advice. This is an educational tool trained on a small dataset. It cannot diagnose or rule out heart disease. Speak to a qualified clinician about any health concern.
